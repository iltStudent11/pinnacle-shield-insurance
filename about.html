About Pinnacle Shield Insurance

Our History

Founded in 1992, Pinnacle Shield Insurance began as a small, family-owned agency with a vision to provide reliable and transparent insurance solutions to our community. Over the past three decades, we have grown into a trusted regional provider, serving thousands of individuals and families with a commitment to integrity and personalized service.

Our Focus

At Pinnacle Shield Insurance, our focus is on protecting what matters most to you. We specialize in auto, home, and life insurance, offering tailored coverage options and clear, honest guidance. Our team believes in empowering clients with knowledge, ensuring you understand your policy and the factors that affect your rates.

We are dedicated to continuous improvement, leveraging technology to make the insurance process simple, transparent, and accessible. Our online quote tools, detailed coverage explanations, and responsive support team are all designed to give you peace of mind and confidence in your coverage.

Why Choose Us?

  • Decades of experience and community trust
  • Personalized insurance solutions
  • Transparent pricing and clear explanations
  • Easy online quotes and account management
  • Friendly, knowledgeable support
