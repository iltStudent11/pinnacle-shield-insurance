Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Pinnacle Shield Insurance.

We offer three main types of insurance: auto, home, and life. Our auto insurance covers liability, collision, and comprehensive risks for your vehicle. Home insurance protects your property and belongings against damage, theft, and liability. Life insurance provides financial security for your loved ones in the event of your passing. If you have questions about these products or need help choosing the right coverage, our agents are happy to assist you.

Your premium is calculated based on a variety of factors, including the type and amount of coverage you choose, your claims history, age, location, and other personal details. For example, a higher coverage limit or lower deductible may increase your premium, while a clean claims history or bundling multiple policies can lower it. We use advanced risk assessment models to ensure your premium reflects your individual risk profile. You may also qualify for discounts, such as safe driver, security system, or multi-policy discounts. Our team is always available to review your policy and help you find ways to save.

Yes, you can change your coverage level at any time to better suit your needs. If you want to increase your protection, decrease your premium, or adjust your deductible, simply contact our customer service team. We will review your current policy, explain your options, and help you make changes. Keep in mind that changing your coverage may affect your premium, and some changes may require underwriting approval. We recommend reviewing your policy annually or after major life events, such as buying a new home or car, to ensure your coverage remains appropriate.

Many factors affect your auto insurance rate, including your driving record (accidents, tickets), the make and model of your vehicle, your age, and your location. Insurers also consider how much you drive each year, your credit history (in some states), and your history of insurance claims. For example, drivers with clean records and newer, safer vehicles typically pay less. Installing anti-theft devices or taking defensive driving courses may also qualify you for discounts. Our agents can help you understand which factors apply to you and how to get the best rate.

Yes, having a professionally installed and monitored security system can lower your home insurance premium. Security systems deter theft and can reduce the risk of property damage, making your home less likely to result in a claim. Some insurers also offer discounts for smoke detectors, fire alarms, and sprinkler systems. To receive a discount, provide proof of installation and monitoring to your agent. Ask us about all available safety-related discounts to maximize your savings.

Basic coverage offers essential protection against the most common risks, such as fire, theft, or liability, at an affordable price. Standard coverage builds on this by increasing coverage limits and adding benefits like personal property replacement or additional living expenses. Premium coverage provides the highest limits and the broadest protection, often including extras such as accidental damage, identity theft protection, or coverage for valuable items. Our agents can help you compare options and choose the right level for your needs and budget.

Filing a claim is straightforward. You can start your claim online, by phone, or through our mobile app—whichever is most convenient for you. Be prepared to provide details about the incident, such as the date, location, and a description of what happened. Supporting documents like photos, police reports, or repair estimates can help speed up the process. Once your claim is submitted, our dedicated claims team will review it, keep you updated, and guide you through every step until your claim is resolved. If you have questions at any point, our representatives are here to help.

Yes, your personal information is secure with us. We use advanced encryption and cybersecurity measures to protect your data both in transit and at rest. Our systems are regularly audited for compliance with industry standards and regulations, such as HIPAA and GDPR where applicable. We never sell your information to third parties, and only authorized personnel have access to your records. Your privacy and trust are our top priorities, and we are committed to keeping your information safe at all times.
